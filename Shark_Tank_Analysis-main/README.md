> Exploring pitches, investors and deals from **Shark Tank** to find out which industries attract funding, which sharks invest the most, and what helps entrepreneurs close a deal.

---

## 📌 Project Objective

To analyze Shark Tank pitch and deal data using SQL and answer business questions about:

- Investment trends across seasons
- Most popular industries among entrepreneurs
- Most active sharks (investors)
- Factors that increase an entrepreneur's chance of getting a deal

---

| `SHARK TANK DATAAS.xlsx` | Dataset used for the analysis |

| `README.md` | Project overview |

---

## 🗃️ Dataset Overview

The dataset contains structured information about:

- **Pitch details** – entrepreneur, business idea, funding asked
- **Shark details** – investors and the investments they made
- **Episode details** – air date, episode number, featured pitches
- **Deal details** – amount invested and equity offered

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

1. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** – trends, patterns and interesting facts in the data
2. **Investment Trends** – funding asked vs. funding received over seasons
3. **Industry Analysis** – which sectors pitch the most and secure the most deals
4. **Shark Analysis** – which sharks are the most active investors
5. **Success Factors** – what separates pitches that get a deal from those that do not
